# The Blue Ants — Authentic Photography Shotlist & Staging Guide

This guide enables the business owner or team to capture authentic, high-end catalog photography using a smartphone (iPhone 13+ or equivalent modern Android) in a single afternoon. No expensive studio or AI generation required.

---

## 1. Universal Staging & Studio Setup

- **Backdrop**: One consistent surface for the entire catalog:
  - Recommended: Natural unbleached Belgian linen fabric, or a matte warm-grey architectural plaster board (available at art supply shops).
  - Clean, flat, zero pattern or gloss.
- **Lighting**:
  - 100% natural indirect window daylight (shoot between 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM near a north or east-facing window).
  - 45-degree side lighting to create gentle, soft contact shadows that give physical depth to boxes and pens.
  - **Strict rule**: Turn off all room overhead fluorescent/LED lights. Never use camera flash.
- **Camera Settings**:
  - Mode: Standard Photo (2x optical zoom to avoid wide-angle lens distortion).
  - Focus: Tap specifically on the foil logo or primary texture (e.g. leather grain).
  - Exposure: Slightly lower exposure (-0.3 to -0.7 EV) to keep whites rich and prevent gold foil highlights from blowing out.
  - Clean the camera lens with a microfiber cloth before shooting.

## 3. Brand Craft & Macro Detail Shots (3–4 Lifestyle/Feature Shots)

Aspect Ratio: **16:9 Landscape (3840 x 2160 px)** for editorial feature blocks and hero backdrops.

1. **Detail Shot A: The Foil Stamp Macro**
   - **Angle**: Extreme close-up macro (2–3 inches away) at 30°.
   - **Subject**: Custom 24k gold foil corporate monogram pressed deep into rigid 1200gsm navy box board.
   - **Look**: Crisp indented paper fibers around the mirror-sheen gold foil edge.

2. **Detail Shot B: The Unboxing Ribbon & Seal**
   - **Angle**: 45° close-up.
   - **Subject**: Hands untying a double-faced grosgrain navy ribbon with a custom embossed wax seal.
   - **Look**: Tactile fabric texture, soft focus background.

3. **Detail Shot C: Foam Inlay Precision**
   - **Angle**: Top-down 45°.
   - **Subject**: High-density die-cut foam bed custom contoured to snugly cradle individual items.
   - **Look**: Precision clean edges, flawless fit showing corporate bespoke engineering.

4. **Detail Shot D: Multi-Suite Stacking (Dispatch Ready)**
   - **Angle**: Low 15° tabletop perspective.
   - **Subject**: 4 finished, ribbon-tied luxury boxes stacked neatly with individual delivery tags.
   - **Look**: Uniformity, perfection at scale, ready for white-glove corporate dispatch.

## 4. Post-Processing Rules (Keep It Authentic)

1. **Do Not Apply Filters**: No Instagram filters, warm yellow tint overlays, or fake HDR.
2. **Crop & Straighten**: Ensure box edges are perfectly vertical and horizontal.
3. **Export Formats**:
   - Master archive: High-resolution PNG/JPEG (original).
   - Production web: Modern WebP / AVIF at 82% quality (dimensions: 800x800 for square cards, 1600x900 for features).
   - Filenames: `assets/products/blueants_[slug].webp` matching the catalog IDs.
